BVLGARI Dashboard v6.1 - Ranking behavior update
=================================================

New ranking formula
-------------------
Final Ranking Score = 0.50 * Model Probability + 0.50 * Scenario Fit

Scenario Fit
------------
Scenario Fit = ProfileWeight * ProfileFit
             + ContentWeight * ContentFit
             + HistoryWeight * CampaignHistoryScore
HistoryWeight = 1 - ProfileWeight - ContentWeight

Behavior
--------
- Brand <-> Campaign changes Profile Fit and can affect both Model Probability and Scenario Fit.
- Profile / Content sliders directly change Scenario Fit and therefore can re-order the ranking.
- Campaign History weight is automatic and changes as the remainder.
- Minimum Followers / Minimum Engagement filter the candidate set.
- Suitable Threshold and Tree Depth remain Decision Tree interpretation controls; they do not directly enter Final Ranking Score.

UI
--
- Top 3 cards show Final Ranking Score as the primary percentage.
- Top 10 list is sorted by Final Ranking Score.
- Model Probability and Scenario Fit remain visible for transparency.

Academic caveat
---------------
The content/history variables and campaign-success target are simulated prototype data.
